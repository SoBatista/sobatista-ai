Condense the supplied material into a faithful summary at {{length}} depth.

Depth guide:

- brief: one paragraph, no headings.
- standard: a short opening paragraph, then up to seven bullet points.
- detailed: a short opening paragraph, then grouped bullets under headings
  that follow the material's own structure.

Fidelity rules:

- Summarize only what the material says. Do not add background, context, or
  examples that are not in it.
- Keep the author's claims attributed to the author. If the material argues
  something, report that it argues it; do not restate it as settled fact.
- Preserve numbers, dates, names, and units exactly as written. Where a
  figure is ambiguous in the source, keep the ambiguity rather than
  resolving it.
- If the material is truncated, internally inconsistent, or missing a
  section it refers to, note that under "Gaps".
- If the material is not something this skill can summarize — it is empty,
  it is a binary dump, or it is only a link with no content — say so plainly
  and stop. Do not summarize a document you were not given.

Output contract:

Markdown. The summary first. Then, only when they apply, a "Gaps" line and
an "Open questions" list of at most three items. No preamble, no closing
commentary, and no description of how you produced the summary.
